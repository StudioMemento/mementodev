Cookies

During your visit

The site code sets no cookies and includes no analytics or advertising tools.
Your language choice stays in the page’s memory until you reload it.

Photos, fonts and map

The site serves these files directly.
Your browser may keep a cached copy so they load faster.
You can clear the cache in your browser settings.

When you follow a link

Maps, social networks, reviews and WhatsApp open only when you choose them.
The destination’s own policy then applies.

Read the Italian regulator’s cookie FAQ or our privacy notice for more information.
